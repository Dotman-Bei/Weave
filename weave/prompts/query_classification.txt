Classify a question asked of a long-term conversational memory system into
exactly one retrieval category.

Categories:
- temporal: asks when something happened, what was said in a particular session,
  or how something changed over time. Chronology is the point of the question.
- preference: asks what the user likes, prefers, wants, or would choose.
- factual: asks for a stable attribute or piece of information about the user or
  a thing they mentioned.
- procedural: asks how to do something, the steps in a process, or a workflow the
  user described.

Answer with a single lowercase word: temporal, preference, factual, or procedural.
